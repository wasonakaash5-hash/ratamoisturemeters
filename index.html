Product divisions
Read our story

For over three decades, Indosaw has supplied science and physics laboratory equipment to schools and colleges, helping institutions build hands-on, rigorous learning environments.

Alongside education, our agricultural division manufactures grain testing and processing equipment used across the post-harvest supply chain — from testing labs to processing facilities.

Both divisions share the same engineering discipline: instruments that are accurate, durable, and built for daily use in demanding settings, exported to customers beyond India as well.
