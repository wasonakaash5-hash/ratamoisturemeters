Our Story

Indosaw was founded in 1993 to bring reliable, well-calibrated instruments to two sectors that can't tolerate error: education and agriculture.

What began as a maker of physics and science laboratory apparatus grew, over three decades, into a group of specialised divisions — each focused on a distinct set of customers, but built on the same manufacturing discipline.

Today, Indosaw Edu supplies science and physics laboratory equipment to schools, colleges and training institutions. Indosaw Agri manufactures grain and seed testing and post-harvest processing equipment for the agricultural supply chain. Our instrumentation line, sold under the Contrex brand, produces rheostats used in laboratories and industrial settings. And through a partnership with Inspire Physics, our education equipment reaches classrooms in the UK as well.

Every division is held to the same standard: instruments that are accurate on day one and stay accurate after years of daily use.
