Our Story

Manufacturing moisture analyzers since 1999

A quarter-century of hands-on experience in the agribusiness industry, built one meter at a time.

Where we started

Wason Instruments was established in 1999 as a manufacturer and exporter of grain moisture analyzers. What began as a small operation grew through consistent attention to build quality and after-sales support.

Today, our meters are used daily by mills, traders, and processors across India, handling everything from paddy and wheat to pulses and oilseeds — where an accurate reading directly affects what a trader is willing to pay.

Milestones

How we got here

1999

R. L. Wason & Company is founded in Jalandhar, Punjab, focused on grain moisture analyzers for local mills.

2008

Expanded the product line to include compact models for smaller trading operations.

2015

Opened a second branch in Bardhaman, West Bengal, to better serve eastern India.

2018

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification, formalizing our quality management system.

Today

Manufacturing six meter models with nationwide service training and manuals available to customers.
